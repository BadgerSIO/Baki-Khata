How to Delete Your Account

Option 1: In-App Self-Serve Deletion (Instant)

If you have the Baki Khata application installed on your Android device:

  1. Open the Baki Khata app.
  2. Tap the Settings (সেটিংস) tab from the bottom navigation.
  3. Scroll down to the Account section.
  4. Tap the red Delete Account (অ্যাকাউন্ট ডিলিট করুন) button.
  5. Confirm by tapping Delete Permanently.

Result: Your cloud data on Supabase and all local records on your device are purged instantly, and you are signed out.

Option 2: Web-Based Deletion Request (Without App)

If you no longer have access to the app, you can submit an account deletion request by emailing us directly from your registered Google/Supabase email address:

Request Account Deletion via Email

Alternatively, email badgers.io.team@gmail.com with the subject "Baki Khata Account Deletion Request" and your registered account email.

What Data is Deleted?

  • Customer Profiles: All customer names, phone numbers, and addresses.
  • Transaction Ledger: All recorded credit (Baki), payment entries, running balances, and timestamps.
  • Shop Profile: Shop name, address, phone number, and digital payment configuration (bKash/Nagad/Rocket).
  • Authentication Credentials: Your user identity and login session in Supabase Auth.

Data Retention Timeline

  • In-app deletion: Processed immediately (0 seconds).
  • Email web requests: Verified and permanently purged from our backend database within 48 hours of receipt.
  • No personal ledger data is retained for marketing, analytics, or third-party purposes following deletion.
